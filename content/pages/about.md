# About me

I am a simulation engineer working primarily in **Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)**, with a focus on turning physical problems into practical numerical models.

My work spans **multiphase flows, reacting flows, aerodynamics, particle transport, heat transfer, and engineering simulation**. I work extensively with **OpenFOAM, Python, C++, Gmsh, PyVista, ParaView, and numerical optimisation**.

## What I work on

My interests sit at the intersection of physics, computation, and engineering:

- Computational Fluid Dynamics and numerical modelling
- Multiphase and Eulerian–Lagrangian simulations
- Reacting flows and combustion modelling
- External and internal aerodynamics
- Meshing and geometry automation
- Reduced-order and engineering models
- Design optimisation and parameter studies
- Scientific Python and simulation tooling

I particularly enjoy problems where a simulation is more than just a result — where understanding the **physics, assumptions, numerical method, and limitations** is as important as obtaining the solution.

## This website

I use this site as a technical notebook and portfolio.

The **CFD Cases** section contains selected simulation projects and case studies. These are intended to document the problem, modelling approach, numerical setup, results, and engineering conclusions.

The **Blog** is where I write about tools, methods, implementation details, and things I learn while working on simulation and engineering problems.

I am especially interested in making engineering workflows more **automated, reproducible, and accessible** through Python and open-source tools.
